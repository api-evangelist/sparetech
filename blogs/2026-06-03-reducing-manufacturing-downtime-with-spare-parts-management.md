What is manufacturing downtime? Manufacturing downtime refers to any period when production is stopped or unavailable because equipment, production lines, or systems are not functioning. It can be planned, such as maintenance, cleaning, inspections, or changeovers, or unplanned, such as equipment failures, spare parts shortages, material issues, or system problems.
