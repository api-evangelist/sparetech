What is MRO sourcing? MRO sourcing refers to the processes and strategies used to identify, validate and procure spare parts, tools and services needed to maintain, repair, and operate production equipment and facilities. Unlike direct materials, MRO items are not part of the final product, but they are essential for ensuring equipment uptime and operational continuity.
