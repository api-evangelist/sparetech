# SPARETECH


> SPARETECH is an enterprise MRO (Maintenance, Repair, and Operations) software that enables maintenance and procurement teams in manufacturing to reliably reduce spend and optimize inventory by sourcing spare parts inside or outside their production network and stop buying parts they already have. The software features a global spare parts catalog with over 40 million original manufacturer part data entries from more than 15,000 original manufacturers, suppliers, and distributors. Using proprietary matching technology and AI, SPARETECH delivers automated duplicate detection, data enrichment, obsolescence management, and BOM (Bill of Materials) processing. SPARETECH is a 100% cloud-based SaaS solution, available in 14 languages, compatible with most ERP and CMMS systems, available in the SAP® Store as an official SAP partner, and certified under SOC 2 Type II, and GDPR.


Founded in 2018 in Stuttgart, Germany, by Dr. Lukas Biedermann and Martin Weber, SPARETECH is used by factories in 70 countries. Customers include Nestlé, Bosch, Porsche, BMW, Volkswagen, Sanofi, WEPA, and other global manufacturers. Industries served include automotive (OEM and tiers), food and beverage, pharmaceuticals, electronics, measuring and control technology (including industrial automation), process industries (petroleum, plastics, paint), chemicals and cement, aerospace, wood and paper production, and industrial machinery and engineering. SPARETECH serves large enterprise manufacturers with multiple production sites. Customers typically achieve a 21% average inventory reduction, 45% reduction in unplanned downtime, 57% less manual effort on BOMs and material checks, and 7% lower spare part procurement costs.

## Product Capabilities


SPARETECH's software is built on four core product pillars for intelligent spare parts management, plus an API for ERP/CMMS integration. Every part in the global catalog is assigned a unique SPARETECH ID, providing a single source of truth across the entire spare parts landscape through proprietary matching technology.


- [Global Spare Parts Search](https://sparetech.io/product/global-spare-parts-search) – Search across your own material master and the SPARETECH database using any information, part numbers, descriptions, or technical details, to find the exact part you need. No training required.
- [Automated BOM Check](https://sparetech.io/product/automated-bom-check) – Instantly analyzes spare parts lists, compares them with your material master data and the global catalog, and identifies what you already have. Detects new materials and enriches them with verified data. Shortens processing time by over 50%.
- [Data Lifecycle Management](https://sparetech.io/product/data-lifecycle-management) – Deduplicate, enrich records with original manufacturer information, and proactively manage discontinuations. Supports ECLASS standard for structured, globally recognized spare parts classification. Goes beyond one-time cleansing by supporting ongoing data lifecycle management.
- [Digital Workflow](https://sparetech.io/product/material-creation-workflow) – Create, update, and optimize material master data through customized processes and AI-enhanced recommendations. Features built-in live duplicate checks, multilingual AI data standardization, and the ability to import 100% accurate original manufacturer data into your ERP. Ensures one part, one reference across your entire global production network.
- [API Solution](https://sparetech.io/product/api) – REST API using JWT authentication for seamless, automated data exchange between SPARETECH and ERP/CMMS/MDM systems. Supports material creation, change, and extension intents with bi-directional synchronization. Developed through an official SAP partnership. Includes a sandbox environment for testing. Supports SAP S/4HANA integration.

## Frequently Asked Questions


### What is SPARETECH?
SPARETECH is an enterprise MRO software that enables maintenance and procurement teams in manufacturing to reliably reduce spend and optimize inventory by sourcing spare parts inside or outside their production network and stop buying parts they already have. It leverages the industry's largest spare part catalog and proprietary matching technology to provide AI recommendations for daily decision-making.


### What makes SPARETECH different from other MRO software?
SPARETECH offers a global spare parts catalog featuring over 40 million original manufacturer part data entries. Each part is assigned a unique SPARETECH ID, giving a single source of truth across the entire spare parts landscape. Using proprietary matching technology and advanced AI techniques, SPARETECH accurately matches customer data to this catalog. Additionally, by connecting all stakeholders in the spare parts ecosystem, SPARETECH enables data sharing, collaboration, and network-driven value.


### Which industries does SPARETECH serve?
Large enterprise manufacturers with multiple production sites across automotive (OEM and tiers), food and beverage, pharmaceuticals, electronics, measuring and control technology (including industrial automation), process industries (petroleum, plastics, paint), chemicals and cement, aerospace, wood and paper production, and industrial machinery and engineering.


### Does SPARETECH replace my ERP system?
No. SPARETECH works as a filter before spare parts data enters your ERP system. It ensures that only error-free information is imported, and it cleans and enriches data already present in the ERP with original manufacturer information. SPARETECH is compatible with most ERP and CMMS systems that allow exporting master data records.


### Does SPARETECH integrate with SAP S/4HANA?
Yes. SPARETECH integrates seamlessly with SAP S/4HANA through secure interfaces, enabling automated data transfer and real-time synchronization for material master data. SPARETECH is an official SAP partner and is available in the SAP® Store.


### How does SPARETECH use AI?
SPARETECH combines proprietary AI models built for industrial spare parts with advanced matching algorithms, forming an expert system refined over years. With an accuracy-first approach, AI is used to eliminate errors, ensure duplicate detection, obsolete part identification, and accurate data enrichment. Generative AI is applied strategically in controlled processes where creativity adds value without risking accuracy.


### What is SPARETECH's pricing model?
Pricing is tailored to business size and scope of use, with no setup or implementation fees. The pricing is fully transparent with no hidden costs. After an initial demo, SPARETECH provides a free proof of value analysis and detailed value quantification report showing potential savings.




### Is SPARETECH cloud-based?
Yes. SPARETECH is a 100% cloud-based SaaS solution available in 14 languages, enabling smooth global rollout across plants in different countries.


### How secure is SPARETECH?
SPARETECH operates under SOC 2 certifications and ensures GDPR compliance. All data is hosted in secure, certified data centers and encrypted during transfer and storage.
